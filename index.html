Our Story

A Legacy of Light

Selvi Fireworks Industries is a proud family-owned business rooted in Sivakasi — India's fireworks capital — run by the Elangovan family. For decades, we have been crafting premium-quality fireworks that light up celebrations across the nation.

From humble beginnings in Sivakasi's vibrant fireworks belt, we have grown into a trusted manufacturer known for uncompromising quality, safety compliance, and a passion for bringing joy to every celebration — big or small.
