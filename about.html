Our Heritage

The Elangovan Family Story

Selvi Fireworks Industries was born in the golden soil of Sivakasi — a city that has lit up the world's celebrations for over a century. Founded by Mr. Elangovan, our business began as a passionate pursuit of crafting fireworks that would bring genuine joy and wonder to people across India. What started as a small manufacturing unit has blossomed into a trusted name in the fireworks industry.

The Elangovan family has poured its heart, expertise, and traditions into every product that leaves our facility. We believe that fireworks are not merely products — they are emotions. They are the light in a child's eyes on Diwali night, the grandeur of a wedding celebration, and the pride of a community coming together in joy.

Today, led by the second generation of the Elangovan family, we continue to honour our founder's vision: to deliver the highest quality fireworks at the most competitive prices, with an unwavering commitment to safety and customer satisfaction. From our factory in Sivakasi, we serve customers across all 28 states of India.

Our Roots

Sivakasi — India's Fireworks Capital

Sivakasi, nestled in the Virudhunagar district of Tamil Nadu, is the undisputed fireworks capital of India. The city produces over 90% of India's fireworks and has earned a legendary reputation the world over for the craftsmanship, creativity, and passion of its manufacturers.

It is here, in this vibrant and storied city, that Selvi Fireworks Industries has its roots. Our facility is located at Srinivasa Nagar, Sattur Main Road — in the heart of Sivakasi's fireworks belt. We are surrounded by generations of knowledge, tradition, and expertise that inform every product we craft.
